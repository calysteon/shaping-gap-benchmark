Research checkpoint history
===========================

02 — Scalable evaluation and verified functional construction
-----------------------------------------------------------

November 12, 2026. Separate follow-on paper by Nathaniel Oh and Paul Attie.
Response-moment pair evaluation; banks through 512 components; common-contract
construction study; fixed-program Boolean synthesis; acquisition-inclusive
work, memory and time accounting; 768 independently verified programs.
See `Checkpoint 02 <checkpoints/02-scaling-and-functions/README.rst>`_.

01 — Effect interfaces and native construction
----------------------------------------------

November 12, 2026. Foundational paper by Nathaniel Oh and Paul Attie.
Accumulated effects and incoming-context partials; acquired interfaces over
22 original components; nine construction controls on 288 later contracts;
native and acquired-bank replay. Publication commit
`20b5a2a <https://github.com/calysteon/shaping-gap-benchmark/commit/20b5a2a8d63998b13e78041d0d3b7ec7812779e7>`_.
See `Checkpoint 01 <checkpoints/01-effect-interfaces/README.rst>`_.
