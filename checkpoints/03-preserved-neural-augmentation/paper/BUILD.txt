Build Paper III from paper/source/main.tex with a standard TeX installation
including geometry, Latin Modern, AMS packages, booktabs, graphicx, microtype
and hyperref. For example, from paper/source:

  latexmk -pdf main.tex

The recorded PDF was compiled with Tectonic 0.17.0. Generated table and figure
inputs are included; regenerate them with code/report.py --paper-assets
/path/to/paper/source. The explicit Latin Modern font declarations accommodate
the recorded minimal cached TeX bundle. They also use standard TeX font names.
